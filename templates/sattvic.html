🌱 Sattvic Principles

  • Prefer minimally processed, varied foods.
  • Keep added oil and salt modest rather than eliminating essential nutrients.
  • Include adequate protein and fibre.
  • Choose regular meals and hydration instead of extreme restriction.
  • Use your saved allergies and food preferences when choosing recipes.
Wellness note: The Sattvic pathway is a lifestyle feature and does not diagnose, treat or guarantee weight loss. Sunlight is not a weight-loss treatment. Stop outdoor exposure if you feel unwell and follow appropriate sun-safety measures.
